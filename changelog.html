NeoJenny changelog

ChangelogNewest first

2026-11-12Download rolled back

  • The download is back to the version from before v1.1.0 while visual problems on Galath and Manglelie are fixed. If you installed v1.1.0 or v1.1.1, download again and replace the jar.

2026-11-12v1.1.1Textures back to normal

  • Texture smoothing is gone: on some girls it streaked the hair with what looked like cobwebs. The textures look exactly as they did before v1.1.0 again.
  • Bees no longer die of fall damage, for example when a world is loaded while one is in the air.
  • Update check: when a newer version is out, a message in chat says so when you join a world, with a link to the download, and the Mods menu marks the mod. The chat message can be turned off with updateNotice = false in config/sexmod-client.toml.
  • No more "custom models" messages in chat when the game starts.

2026-11-11v1.1.0NeoJenny: smoother textures and jiggle physics

  • The mod is now called NeoJenny.
  • Smoother textures: the blocky pixel shading on hair and clothes becomes soft gradients, while faces, outlines and colour edges stay sharp. The girls no longer flicker and look jagged from a distance.
  • Jiggle physics for every girl: chests and butts move like soft flesh (each side on its own), tails and ponytails swing. On during scenes; outside scenes it is off by default, and can be turned on to a chosen share.
  • In scenes, wherever the animation already moves the chest or butt by hand, the physics leaves it to the animation; on hard, fast scenes the bounce is evened out so nothing twitches or snaps.
  • Both can be turned off, and the jiggle made stronger or weaker, in config/sexmod-client.toml.
  • A small link to the Discord in the corner of the title screen (can be hidden in the same config file).
  • The "loading up custom models" messages no longer show up in chat every time the game starts.
  • The girls' houses no longer sit on a one-block ledge of dirt on gently uneven ground: they stand at the height most of the ground under them has.
  • Trees no longer grow through the girls' houses: world generation keeps them a few blocks away.

2026-11-06Galath riding and goblin fixes

  • Galath no longer repeats her kill line over and over after she kills you.
  • Riding Galath when she has Manglelie: you can steer from the start (no boost needed first), and she no longer fights mobs while you ride her.
  • Boosting into the ground no longer kills you with fall damage.
  • No more camera flicker while flying on Galath, and Manglelie no longer pops in and out during a boost.
  • A thrown goblin now flies off from your hands instead of hanging in front of you and then teleporting to where she landed.

2026-11-05Dragon Staff

  • Beds and chests can be assigned to Kobold tribes with the Dragon Staff again.

2026-11-02Dragon Staff

  • The staff no longer stays bound to a tribe whose Kobolds have all died, so you can start a new tribe.

2026-11-01First public release

  • A full rewrite of FapCraft 1.12.2 for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge: all eleven companions with their AI, menus, scenes, dialogue and sounds; Kobold tribes, goblin lairs and the Galath boss fight; player-as-girl forms, custom models and clothing, Allie's lamp and the Dragon Staff; the original houses and lairs as real 1.21 structures.
